Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy covers the Reactor Run app for iPhone and Android. The developer can be reached at theskiingfox@gmail.com.

What this version collects

Reactor Run does not create an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, or location. It does not show ads. It does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not use advertising or analytics services. The developer does not receive a copy of your play.

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following in local storage on the device:

  • Best scores, and the ship and level that set each one
  • Ships you build
  • Worlds and upgrades you have unlocked
  • Settings, including a pilot name if you set one

That information stays on the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no server holding it for this version.

Purchases

Optional unlocks are sold by Apple on iPhone and by Google on Android. Apple or Google takes the payment. Reactor Run does not receive your card number, billing address, or account password. The store tells the app, on the device, which products that account owns, so the app can unlock them.

Opening every world, and the special paints, trims, and engine glows, are remembered by the store. Speed, handling, and boost upgrades are remembered on the phone, for the ship they were bought for.

Photos and sharing

The share button makes a picture of a ship on the phone and opens the system share sheet. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks permission to add that picture to your photo library. The app does not upload the picture. Anything you then send is handled by the app you chose in the share sheet, under that app’s own policy.

Worldwide scores

The score table in this version is on your phone only. A later version may add a worldwide table. Before any version sends a pilot name, a score, a ship, or an anonymous id off the device, this policy will be updated on this page.

Children

The app does not ask children for personal information and it has no account to register.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page, with a new date at the top.
